# Daftarche

A centralized contact and interaction management tool for the Dataist ecosystem.

Daftarche brings scattered contact details—such as phone numbers, addresses, and communication records—into a single, structured, and privacy-conscious store, while supporting synchronization with other Dataist modules.

## Key Features

- **Centralized Management:** Keep contact and communication details organized in one consistent structure.
- **Fast Search:** Quickly find contact information through a streamlined interface.
- **Data-First Privacy:** Store contact data with security and privacy practices in mind.
- **Ecosystem Compatibility:** Exchange and sync information with other tools across the Dataist ecosystem.

## Self-Knowledge & Network Insights

Analyzing your communication network supports data-driven self-knowledge. Daftarche helps you spot interaction patterns, see who plays a key role in your network, and understand your social environment while respecting personal privacy.
